For Apollo, DI&#160;lines are called ticketing remark lines. These lines contain information such as price, flight information, confirmation, ticketing time limits, fees, and form of payment. DI lines display in sequential order with the oldest entries at the top and the most recent entries at the bottom of the list. The DI&#160;lines include an NDC identifier per below to differentiate NDC bookings from low-cost carrier (LCC) or GDS bookings: DOCI-FREE TEXT-#TAS01#REFXX/5AAAAA/AUD/ NDC DI&#160;Line Examples (1G) The examples in this section are for NDC content from Galileo. For NDC content from Apollo (1V) and Worldspan (1P) , see MIR and TAIR Examples and note the following: For Apollo, the DI.FT- prefix is replaced by T . DI&#160;lines in Apollo are called ticketing remarks . For Worldspan, the DI.FT- prefix is replaced by 5.z as well as a -- at the end, which is joined with the next line due to the number of characters limitation.
